Privacy Policy for GK Buddy AI

Last updated: September 7, 2026

GK Buddy AI is an AI-powered general knowledge and educational assistant. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the GK Buddy AI website or Android application.

1. Information We Process

When you ask a question, the text you submit may be sent to our backend server and to third-party AI services so that GK Buddy AI can generate a response.

For questions requiring current information, GK Buddy AI may use a web search service to obtain relevant search results.

Conversation information may be temporarily retained in server memory while the server is running so that conversation context can be maintained. This temporary conversation data is not intended to be permanent storage and may be lost when the server restarts.

2. Advertising

GK Buddy AI displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and usage information, depending on applicable settings, consent, and regional requirements.

You can learn more about Google's advertising and privacy practices through Google's privacy information and advertising settings.

3. Third-Party Services

GK Buddy AI may use third-party services including:

  • Google Gemini for AI-generated responses.
  • Tavily for web search results for questions requiring current information.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for displaying advertisements.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Children's Privacy

GK Buddy AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, or other confidential information through the service.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the service. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

6. Data Retention

Conversation information used to maintain chat context may be stored temporarily in server memory. We do not intend for this temporary conversation storage to serve as a permanent user database.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please use the support contact provided on the official Google Play listing for GK Buddy AI.
